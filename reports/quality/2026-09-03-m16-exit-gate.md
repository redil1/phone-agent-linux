## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone 16 provides multi-tenant isolation, role-based access control (RBAC), and cryptographically chained immutable audit logging (`ai_bridge/enterprise_security.py`). Administrative operations are partitioned by role (`admin`, `operator`, `auditor`, `caller`), and every access decision is permanently recorded in a tamper-evident SHA-256 hash chain.

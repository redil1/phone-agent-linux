## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone 10 delivers tenant-isolated knowledge ingestion and grounded retrieval (`ai_bridge/knowledge_retrieval.py`). The retrieval engine enforces strict multi-tenant namespace isolation, prevents cross-tenant data leakage, and automatically suppresses deactivated or expired facts.

---

## 2. Deliverables & Technical Enforcements

1. **Structured Product Ontology & Grounded Facts (`M10-03`, `M10-04`):**
   - `GroundedFact` captures fact identifiers, tenant scope, topic classifications, content, active status, and trust levels.
2. **Tenant-Isolated Retrieval (`M10-05`, `M10-06`):**
   - `KnowledgeRetrievalEngine` ensures queries for a tenant only inspect and retrieve facts belonging to that tenant.
3. **Suppression of Deactivated/Expired Facts (`M10-09`):**
   - Inactive facts (`is_active=False`) are never returned in search results.
4. **Automated Verification:**
   - Unit tests in `tests/test_knowledge_retrieval.py` verify isolation, query matching, and inactive suppression.

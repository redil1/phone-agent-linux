## Rollback proof

`qualification.initial_baseline_rollback` now owns exact-image rollback. It refuses an active call,
missing/mismatched baseline image, mutable image substitution, wrong Compose selection, unhealthy
worker, stale configuration, or provider/model mismatch. It changes only the core service, never
builds or pulls during rollback, preserves state mounts, and restores the previous image if the
target fails.

Five focused tests prove validation-only behavior, candidate-to-baseline restoration, active-call
refusal, failed-target recovery, and configuration-drift rejection. The live `--apply-runtime`
drill passed 15 checks and correctly performed no mutation because the exact frozen image was
already healthy. Android recovery boundaries, hashes, preflights, and operator-authority rules are
documented in `docs/M0_INITIAL_BASELINE_ROLLBACK.md`; no phone flash or APK uninstall was needed or
performed.
